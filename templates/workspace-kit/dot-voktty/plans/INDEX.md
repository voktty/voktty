# Plan index

The single index of planned work. How a plan is written and run:
`README.md`. When someone asks "what is left?", the answer comes from this
index, `TRACKING.md`, the newest handoffs and the affected plan.

States: **open** (in progress or with pending milestones) · **written** (not
started) · **waiting** (blocked on {owner_title} or a third party) ·
**parked** · **closed**.
